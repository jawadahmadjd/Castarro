Recommendation

Use the recommended tweak if you want the interface to feel clearer at a glance. The original is cohesive, but the orange accent is close enough to the red tones that live, primary, and selected states can visually compete. The softer peach accent keeps the same warm identity while making selected controls and focus states feel less aggressive.
